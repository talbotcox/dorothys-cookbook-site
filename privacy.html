Privacy

Effective 9 November 2026

Dorothy's Cookbook is made by Talbot Cox for his family. There are no ads, no tracking and no analytics, and nothing is ever sold or shared with advertisers.

Without signing in

You can read and cook every recipe without an account. Your shopping list, timers and the step you're cooking stay on your device. If you choose, the shopping list is copied into the Reminders app on your device.

If you sign in

Signing in (with Apple or by email) lets you join the family features. We then store:

  • your name and email address (or the private address Apple gives us), and an account ID;
  • what you add: hearts, notes, "I made this" photos, recipes you share, and any reports or blocks you make.

This is kept with Supabase, our database host, in the United States, and is used only to run the app. Notes you mark private are visible only to you. Shared items are visible to family members the app owner has approved. New accounts see nothing shared until they are approved. The full family tree and birthdays are shown only to approved family members.

Keeping and deleting

We keep your information until you delete it. In the app, tap your profile, then Delete my account and everything I added: your account and everything you added are deleted straight away. You can also email us to ask.

Children

Accounts are for adults and teens. A child under 13 should not create an account; parents can share for them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will change too. Questions: talbotcoxmedia@gmail.com.
