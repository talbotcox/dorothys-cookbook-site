Dorothy's Cookbook

A grandmother's recipe book, kept for the family

Dorothy's Cookbook holds Dorothy Cox's handwritten recipes with step-by-step cooking, timers and a shopping list, plus a place for her family to share their own recipes, photos and notes.

Getting help

Questions, problems or ideas: email talbotcoxmedia@gmail.com. We usually reply within a day or two.

Reporting something

Anything family members share can be reported from inside the app (tap Report on the post), and you can block a person so you no longer see what they share. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours, and anything objectionable is removed. You can also email us.

Deleting your account

In the app, tap your profile, then Delete my account and everything I added. Your profile, notes, hearts, photos and shared recipes are deleted straight away. Or email us and we'll do it for you.
